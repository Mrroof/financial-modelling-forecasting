# Financial Modelling and Forecasting

## Objective
Build a three-scenario operating model to explore how deposit growth, lending growth, pricing and operating costs can affect illustrative bank income.

## Included workbook
`model/financial_scenario_model.xlsx`

## Data notice
All opening balances and assumptions are **illustrative assumptions**, not actual bank figures or forecasts. Replace them with authorized, sourced inputs before drawing business conclusions.

## Workbook structure
- Assumptions: downside, base and upside drivers.
- Operating Model: forecast balances, income and expense calculations.
- Summary: key outputs.

## How to use
Open the workbook in Excel. Change scenario assumptions and inspect the operating model and summary. Formula cells recalculate in Excel.

## Analytical considerations
Document the source and date of each assumption, distinguish nominal from real growth, and test sensitivity to rates, funding costs, impairment and operating expenses.
